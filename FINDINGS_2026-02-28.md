## 1. LIGO Quasi-Normal Mode Analysis

### 1a. DSI Search (Test A: Frequency Ratios)

**Data:** 14 confident binary black hole merger events from GWTC-1/2/3,
yielding 90 pairwise QNM frequency ratios.

**Method:** Rayleigh test for uniformity of `ln(f_i/f_j) mod ln(φ)` phase
distribution. If QNM frequencies are quantized at golden-ratio spacings,
the phases cluster rather than being uniform.

**Result:** Significant clustering detected — but subsequently invalidated
by null tests (see below).

### 1b. LIGO Validation Tests

| Test | Method | Result |
|------|--------|--------|
| Detector split (H1 vs L1) | Separate periodograms per detector | Both show >3σ at m=1,2,3 — **consistent between detectors** |
| Spectral line masking | Remove known instrumental lines, re-analyze | >3σ persists at m=1,2,3 — **survives masking** |
| **Off-source null test** | Same analysis on noise segments away from events | **>3σ persists in off-source data** |

**Verdict: ❌ Systematic artifact.** The "signal" appears equally strongly in
off-source noise, proving it is an instrumental/systematic feature of LIGO's
noise floor, not an astrophysical signal. The m=1 harmonic deviates 24% from
the target frequency, further indicating it is not the predicted φ-signature.

**Lesson learned:** LIGO's noise power spectral density has broad structure
that can mimic periodic features. Any future LIGO test must subtract the
noise PSD model before searching for φ-signatures.

## 2. CMB Power Spectrum Analysis (Planck 2018)

### 2a. Full Unbinned Lomb-Scargle in ln(ℓ)

**Data:** Planck 2018 unbinned CMB spectra — TT (2,507 multipoles, ℓ = 2–2508),
TE (1,995 multipoles, ℓ = 2–1996), EE (1,995 multipoles, ℓ = 2–1996).

**Method:** Subtract Planck best-fit ΛCDM theory (corrected by calPlanck²),
compute Lomb-Scargle periodogram of residuals in ln(ℓ) space. Target:
f_φ = 1/ln(φ) ≈ 2.078 cycles per unit ln(ℓ). Significance via 300 and
10,000 bootstrap shuffles.

**Results — Harmonics at f_φ:**

| Spectrum | m=1 (f ≈ 2.08) | m=2 (f ≈ 4.16) | m=3 (f ≈ 6.23) | m=4 (f ≈ 8.31) |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| **TT** | <1σ (p=1.0) | <1σ (p=1.0) | <1σ (p=1.0) | <1σ (p=1.0) |
| **TE** | <1σ (p=1.0) | <1σ (p=1.0) | <1σ (p=1.0) | <1σ (p=1.0) |
| **EE** | <1σ (p=1.0) | <1σ (p=1.0) | <1σ (p=1.0) | <1σ (p=1.0) |

**Cross-spectrum periodogram correlations:** TT–TE: 0.15, TT–EE: 0.001, TE–EE: −0.01

**Verdict:** No significant DSI evidence in any CMB channel. All four harmonics
are below 1σ in all three spectra. No correlated signal across spectra.

### 2b. CMB DSI Log-Periodic Fit (Dedicated ln(ℓ) Analysis)

**Data:** Planck TT, ℓ = 30–2500 (avoiding low-ℓ cosmic variance).

## 3. Systematic φ^m Power Scan

**Method:** For each channel (CMB TT, TE, EE; DESI BAO; LIGO QNM), fit a
2-harmonic log-periodic template with fundamental period m·ln(φ) for
m = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. This tests whether the signal might appear at a
higher power of φ rather than the fundamental.

### Best Results per Channel

| Channel | Best m | Δχ² | p-value | Significance | |ε₁| |
|---------|--------|------|---------|--------------|------|
| CMB TT | φ³ | 2.7 | 0.615 | −0.3σ | 0.0018 |
| CMB TE | φ⁴ | 4.0 | 0.409 | 0.2σ | 0.0357 |
| CMB EE | φ² | 3.4 | 0.486 | 0.0σ | 0.0087 |
| DESI BAO | φ⁶ | 15.9 | 0.003 | **2.7σ** | 0.0364 |
| LIGO QNM | φ³ | 158.3 | ~0 | **>8σ** | 1.2992 |

**Notes:**
- CMB channels show no significant preference for any φ^m period.
- DESI BAO shows a 2.7σ preference for φ⁶ (period ≈ 2.89 in ln(s)).
- LIGO QNM shows extreme significance at all m values — this is the
  systematic artifact identified in the null tests (Section 1b). The
  |ε₁| > 1 values are unphysical (>100% modulation), confirming the
  signal is instrumental, not cosmological.

### φ⁴ and φ⁵ Multi-Harmonic Template Tests

Dedicated 4- and 5-harmonic template fits were run with phase-free fitting:

**φ⁵ test:** CMB TT Δχ² = 3.8 (−1.7σ), TE Δχ² = 6.7 (−0.7σ), EE Δχ² = 5.7 (−1.0σ), LIGO R = 0.06 (−0.6σ). No signal.

**φ⁴ test:** CMB TT Δχ² = 3.5 (−1.3σ), TE Δχ² = 5.4 (−0.6σ), EE Δχ² = 4.6 (−0.9σ), DESI Δχ² = 8.5 (0.3σ), LIGO R = 0.10 (0.2σ). No signal.

## Key Findings

### 1. The CMB provides the cleanest null result

With 2,507 unbinned multipoles and three independent spectra (TT, TE, EE),
the Planck data show **zero evidence** for φ-log-periodic modulations at any
harmonic (m = 1–6) or power (φ¹ through φ⁶). All p-values are 1.0, meaning
the actual power at f_φ is below typical noise realizations. This is the
strongest constraint on the hypothesis.

**Upper bound on CMB modulation amplitude:** |ε_CMB| < 0.002 (TT), < 0.01 (TE),
< 0.01 (EE) — any quasicrystal imprint on the CMB must be smaller than ~0.2%
of the TT power spectrum.

### 2. LIGO results are entirely systematic

The dramatic >3σ signals seen in LIGO QNM analysis were proven to be
instrumental artifacts by the off-source null test. The signal appears
identically in noise-only data segments. Additionally, best-fit amplitudes
|ε| > 1 are unphysical. **All LIGO results should be disregarded.**

### 3. BAO shows mixed signals — one intriguing outlier

The single-bin DESI DR1 LRG (z = 0.6–0.8) full-shape analysis shows a
3.2σ improvement when adding a φ-log-periodic modulation (Δχ² = 15.7 for
3 extra parameters, AIC favored). However:

- The multi-bin analysis with a standardized pipeline shows no
  significant signal in the same bin (Δχ² = 0.29) — sensitivity depends
  heavily on fitting methodology
- The combined 6-bin Fisher test gives 0.14σ — no overall signal
- The ELG bin's 1.9σ result fails its own null test (random phases fit equally well)
- DESI DR2 compressed BAO favors w₀wₐ over DSI by Δχ² = 6.2

### 4. No channel shows a consistent φ-signature

The hypothesis predicts the same fundamental frequency f_φ = 1/ln(φ) ≈ 2.078
should appear across all cosmological probes. No channel shows even a 2σ
signal at this frequency. The various marginal hints (BAO single-bin, CMB TE
at φ⁴) are at different frequencies and do not corroborate each other.

## Honest Assessment

After testing the Gravitotemporal Quasicrystal Hypothesis against five
independent data channels (Pantheon+ SNe Ia, Planck CMB TT/TE/EE, LIGO QNM,
DESI BAO full-shape and compressed), the results are:

- **Zero positive detections** at the predicted fundamental frequency f_φ ≈ 2.078
- **One systematic artifact** (LIGO) that initially appeared significant but
  was invalidated by null tests
- **One intriguing outlier** (BAO single-bin at 3.2σ) that does not replicate
  across bins or fitting methodologies
- **Tight upper bounds** on CMB modulation amplitude (~0.2% of the power spectrum)

The hypothesis is not falsified — Model C remains consistent with ΛCDM
(ε = 0 is within 1σ of all constraints). But it has produced no positive
evidence across any probe tested. The framework's predictions are currently
indistinguishable from standard ΛCDM at the sensitivity of available data.

### What would change this assessment

- A >3σ detection at f_φ ≈ 2.078 in **multiple independent channels**
- Consistent ε > 0 across CMB, BAO, and future probes
- A signal that passes null tests (off-source, random phase, scrambled data)

### Remaining avenues

- **DESI DR2 full-shape correlation functions** (not yet available as public VACs)
- **SPT-3G / ACT DR6 CMB data** — independent CMB measurements with different systematics
- **Euclid galaxy power spectrum** — millions of galaxies with P(k) measured
  directly, providing the most natural probe for log-periodic modulations
- **21cm intensity mapping** — offers continuous spectral coverage in redshift
